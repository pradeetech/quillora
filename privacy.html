Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Welcome to Quillora ("we," "our," or "us"), accessible from this website. Your privacy is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data. By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide: When you register for an account on Quillora, we collect your name and email address. This is used to create and manage your account, save your bookmarked articles, and communicate with you when necessary.

b) Automatically collected information: When you browse our site, we automatically collect certain non-personal data, including your browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referral source. This helps us understand how visitors use our website and improve our content.

c) Cookies: We use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to enhance your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and analyze site traffic.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in the following ways:

• To operate, maintain, and improve our website and services
• To create and manage your user account
• To save articles you bookmark to your personal reading list
• To send you email verification messages and important account notifications
• To analyze website usage and improve our content quality
• To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse

We never sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising

We may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense to display advertisements on this website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other websites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

• Firebase (Google Cloud): Used for user authentication and secure data storage. Firebase handles your login credentials and account data in compliance with Google's privacy standards.
• Google Analytics: May be used to collect anonymous usage statistics such as page views and visitor behavior.
• Google Search Console: Used to monitor how our website appears in Google search results.

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy, which you may review on their respective websites.

5. Affiliate Disclosure

Some articles on Quillora may contain affiliate links. This means that if you click on a link and make a purchase, we may receive a small commission — at no additional cost to you. Affiliate relationships help us keep Quillora running and continue producing free content. We only recommend products or services we believe provide genuine value to our readers.

6. Data Security

We take the security of your information seriously. Your account data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure), which uses industry-standard encryption and security measures. While no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information.

7. Your Data Rights

You have the right to:

• Access the personal information we hold about you
• Request correction of inaccurate data
• Request deletion of your account and associated data
• Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through our Contact page or email us directly.

8. Children's Privacy

Quillora does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided such information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
